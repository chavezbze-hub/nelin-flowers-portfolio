02 / My approach

Four principles that guide my practice

01 · ACTIVE LISTENING

Active Listening

Empathy and active listening create the beginning of trust. I observe carefully, remain present, and make space for each person’s experience without judgment.

02 · CREATE A SAFE SPACE

Create a Safe Space

A safe space is respectful, culturally competent, trauma-informed, and free from shame. I strive to help people feel heard, valued, and supported as they share their needs and goals.

03 · THERAPEUTIC ALLIANCE

Initiate a Therapeutic Alliance

A therapeutic alliance grows through consistency, honesty, collaboration, and client-centered care. I will work with each person to identify strengths and support meaningful next steps.

04 · CONTINUED EDUCATION

Continued Education

Continued education strengthens ethical practice. I bring curiosity, humility, and a strong commitment to learning into every placement and professional relationship.
